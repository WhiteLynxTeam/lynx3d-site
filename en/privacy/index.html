Lynx3D privacy

No ads, analytics or trackers. You don't need an account to browse.

Only on your phone

Settings, the image and depth map cache, your own depth maps, Civitai sign-in tokens (encrypted with a key in Android Keystore). Depth maps are computed on the phone — images are not uploaded anywhere. App data is excluded from Android backups.

Photos and videos on your phone

The Phone gallery reads photos and videos from the Android media library only with your permission; on Android 14 and newer you can allow just selected photos. Files are not copied or uploaded anywhere: viewing, depth maps and VR all run on the device. A file is opened in another app or shared only when you tap to do so. You can revoke the permission in Android settings.

Civitai

Images and their data load directly from civitai.com. Signing in with Civitai happens on Civitai's own page — your password never passes through the app. After that your reactions, collections and follows are read and changed in your Civitai account under its privacy policy.

Wikimedia Commons

Photos and their details (author, license) load directly from commons.wikimedia.org and upload.wikimedia.org. Wikimedia sees your IP address; see the Wikimedia Foundation privacy policy.

Our server (extended tags)

After you accept the rules, the app creates a guest profile: the public part of a device key (the private key never leaves the phone), phone model and language, your consents with the rules version, a random nickname, your tags, votes and reports with image IDs, saved settings so they come back after reinstalling; and only if you link them — email or Telegram. Like any web server, it keeps technical request logs (time, address, IP) to run the service and protect it from abuse.

Updates and models

The app checks for new versions and downloads depth models (when you tap the button) from GitHub. No personal data is sent; GitHub sees your IP address.

Deleting your data

Settings → App profile → Delete profile deletes your data on our server; tags you added stay without your name. Data on the phone is removed with the app. To disconnect Civitai, sign out in Settings.

This site

Static, with no cookies, analytics or forms. The host sees visitors' IP addresses.
